Golden Shores / Terrain Studio

Edit the collision surfaces directly over the original paintings. Changes are local to this browser until exported and committed.

Game
● Ground● Platform● Wall● Water hazard
Drag a vertex to reposition it. Insert adds a vertex to the selected line; new lines are drawn with clicks from left to right. At joins, the game gently connects touching ground surfaces. Platforms are one-way. Red walls block the crab horizontally. Blue water polygons cause a fall and a lost heart if the crab enters while airborne; leave real gaps in the ground. Draw walls or water vertices in any direction. Export your finished JSON and send it to ChatGPT to publish it for everyone.
